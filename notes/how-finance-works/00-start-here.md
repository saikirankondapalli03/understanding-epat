# How Finance Works — Chapter 1 study path

Book: *How Finance Works: The HBR Guide to Thinking Smart About the Numbers*  
Author: Mihir A. Desai  
Chapter 1: **Financial Analysis** — using ratios to analyze performance, while playing a game

These notes are a beginner’s companion, written in original language. They are **not** a substitute for the book. Play Desai’s matching game in the book itself; that is how the intuition actually forms.

---

## What this chapter is trying to do to you

Most people are taught to *read* financial statements. Desai wants you to *see through* them.

A number by itself is almost never useful. “Profit was $100 million” could mean a triumph or a disaster. You only know after you compare it to something: sales, assets, equity, last year, or a rival in the same industry.

That comparison is a **ratio**. Ratios are the language of finance. Chapter 1 teaches you to speak it.

## The one idea to carry around

> Look at extremes. Invent a story. Then check the story against a second number.

That is financial analysis. Everything else is vocabulary.
